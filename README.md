No one has delivered any mail to the small town of Bluefield in three months, and it's your job to fix that.
Even if some of the mailboxes seem a little hard to get to.
There seem to be some tapes left around the town, too... maybe you can use them to piece together what happened here.

Made for a game design course in 2024.

CONTROLS
WASD to move
Mouse to move camera
Space to jump and progress dialogue
E to interact
ESC to pause
Subtitles can be enabled through the pause menu


Available to play at https://eyeofthedragon.itch.io/communications-delay
